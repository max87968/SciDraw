# Example: Microfluidic Experimental Setup Schematic

**Goal:** Produce a methods-review diagram for a two-inlet microfluidic mixing setup.

## Copyable prompt

```text
Create a clean technical schematic of a conceptual two-inlet microfluidic
mixing experiment. Draw two syringe pumps, each connected to one inlet of a
Y-shaped microfluidic channel. Show the junction, mixing channel, imaging
region, and collection outlet. Label the parts and indicate the direction of
fluid flow with arrows. Use a white background and a precise, minimal layout.
Do not add flow rates, measurements, or sensor specifications that have not
been supplied.
```

## Review checklist

- Every inlet, outlet, and tubing connection is physically plausible.
- Arrow direction matches the specified experiment.
- Measurement and imaging points are placed only where requested.
- The final figure agrees with the actual lab method and setup.
